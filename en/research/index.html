About

I am a master's student in psychology at Nanjing Normal University, advised by Prof. Chuan-Peng Hu. My work sits at the intersection of computational modelling and open science: I build models that describe how people make decisions, and I work on making the data behind those models comparable and reusable across studies.

My current focus is experimental design optimization — using a model of a model to decide which experiment is worth running next, so that the parameters we care about can actually be estimated.

Research Interests

  • Computational modelling of decision-making
  • Drift-diffusion models
  • Bayesian hierarchical modelling
  • Gaussian process surrogates
  • Experimental design optimization
  • Parameter recovery and model validation
  • Self-prioritization effect
  • Open science and data standardization

Research

Optimizing the Experimental Design Space of the Self-Prioritization Effect
Master's thesis · Hu Lab, Nanjing Normal University · 2026 – present
  • Built a generative framework mapping continuous design variables onto drift-diffusion model parameters and then onto trial-level behaviour, reframing discrete condition comparison as modelling of a continuous design space.
  • Fit hierarchical Bayesian drift-diffusion models (HDDM) to real data across eight design configurations; compared models by DIC and ran Bayes-factor prior sensitivity checks.
  • Used Gaussian process surrogate models to predict parameters across the design space, validated with parameter-recovery simulations.
  • Code
The Self-Prioritization Effect Database (SPE-DB)first author
Hu Lab, Nanjing Normal University · 2024 – present
  • Curated a standardized trial-level open database of the self-matching task, integrating 44 studies, 70 experiments, 3,603 participants and over 1.55 million trials.
  • Designed a three-level data architecture (global index to study-level JSON metadata to experiment-level files) and a five-component task-standardization framework.
  • Standardized heterogeneous variables across studies into a unified schema with six identity categories and a published codebook.
  • Ran large-scale analyses feasible only on aggregated data, including linear mixed-effects models with bootstrap resampling over 853,015 nonmatching trials.
Effect of Social Distance on Fairness Preference
Undergraduate thesis · Qinghai Minzu University · 2023 – 2024
  • Designed, programmed, ran and analysed a 3 (social distance) by 5 (offer split) within-subjects Ultimatum Game, programmed from scratch in E-Prime 2.0; 23 participants, 1,800 trials.
  • Code
