<h2 class="acad-h2">About</h2>

<p class="acad-pt">I am a master's student in psychology at Nanjing Normal University, advised by <strong>Prof. Chuan-Peng Hu</strong>. My work sits at the intersection of <strong>computational modelling</strong> and <strong>open science</strong>: I build models that describe how people make decisions, and I work on making the data behind those models comparable and reusable across studies.</p>

<p class="acad-pt">My current focus is <strong>experimental design optimization</strong> — using a model of a model to decide which experiment is worth running next, so that the parameters we care about can actually be estimated.</p>

<h2 class="acad-h2">Research</h2>

<div class="acad-entry">
<div class="acad-etitle">Optimizing the Experimental Design Space of the Self-Prioritization Effect</div>
<div class="acad-emeta">Master's thesis · Hu Lab, Nanjing Normal University · 2026 – present</div>
<ul>
<li>Built a generative framework mapping continuous design variables onto drift-diffusion model parameters and then onto trial-level behaviour, reframing discrete condition comparison as modelling of a continuous design space.</li>
<li>Fit hierarchical Bayesian drift-diffusion models (HDDM) to real data across eight design configurations; compared models by DIC and ran Bayes-factor prior sensitivity checks.</li>
<li>Used <strong>Gaussian process surrogate models</strong> to predict parameters across the design space, validated with parameter-recovery simulations.</li>
<li><a href="https://github.com/Caizhenxin/Guassion_Process-and-Experiment_Design" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Code</a></li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="acad-entry">
<div class="acad-etitle">The Self-Prioritization Effect Database (SPE-DB)<span class="acad-tag">first author</span></div>
<div class="acad-emeta">Hu Lab, Nanjing Normal University · 2024 – present</div>
<ul>
<li>Curated a standardized trial-level open database of the self-matching task, integrating <strong>44 studies, 70 experiments, 3,603 participants and over 1.55 million trials</strong>.</li>
<li>Designed a three-level data architecture (global index to study-level JSON metadata to experiment-level files) and a five-component task-standardization framework.</li>
<li>Standardized heterogeneous variables across studies into a unified schema with six identity categories and a published codebook.</li>
<li>Ran large-scale analyses feasible only on aggregated data, including linear mixed-effects models with bootstrap resampling over 853,015 nonmatching trials.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="acad-entry">
<div class="acad-etitle">Effect of Social Distance on Fairness Preference</div>
<div class="acad-emeta">Undergraduate thesis · Qinghai Minzu University · 2023 – 2024</div>
<ul>
<li>Designed, programmed, ran and analysed a 3 (social distance) by 5 (offer split) within-subjects Ultimatum Game, programmed from scratch in E-Prime 2.0; 23 participants, 1,800 trials.</li>
<li><a href="https://github.com/Caizhenxin/ultimatum-game" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Code</a></li>
</ul>
</div>
